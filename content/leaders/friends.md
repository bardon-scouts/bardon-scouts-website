Friends of Bardon Scouts are people who have been part of our Scout Group and want to stay connected with our community. Whether you're a former leader, parent, committee member, or supporter, we value your ongoing connection to Bardon Scouts.

## Who Are Friends of Bardon Scouts?

Friends of Bardon Scouts include:

- **Former leaders** who have moved on but want to stay in touch
- **Parents** whose children have aged out of Scouting
- **Former committee members** who remain interested in the group's activities
- **Past supporters** who contributed to our group over the years
- **Alumni Scouts** who grew up in Bardon Scouts and want to stay connected
- **Community members** who support our mission and programs

## Staying Connected

As a Friend of Bardon Scouts, you'll:

- Receive invitations to special events like the [Annual Report Presentation](/annual-report-presentation/)
- Stay informed about major group milestones and achievements
- Be welcomed at group celebrations and reunions
- Maintain your connection to the Bardon Scouts community

## Annual Report Presentation

All Friends of Bardon Scouts are warmly invited to attend our Annual Report Presentation each March. This important event provides:

- Updates on section activities and achievements
- Financial reports and group overview
- Future plans and initiatives
- Opportunity to reconnect with the Bardon Scouts community

[Learn more about Annual Report Presentation](/annual-report-presentation/)

## How to Become a Friend

If you'd like to be added to our Friends of Bardon Scouts list to receive event invitations and stay informed about group activities, please [contact us](/contact/) with:

- Your name
- Your previous connection to Bardon Scouts (leader, parent, committee member, etc.)
- Your preferred email address for communications
- Any years you were involved with the group

We'll add you to our Friends list and keep you connected to the Bardon Scouts community.

## Ways to Support

While there's no obligation, Friends of Bardon Scouts can support the group in various ways:

- **Share your expertise** - Offer occasional assistance or advice in your area of expertise
- **Attend events** - Join us at the Annual Report Presentation and special celebrations
- **Spread the word** - Help promote Bardon Scouts to families in your community
- **Stay engaged** - Share your Scouting stories and encourage the next generation

## Volunteering Opportunities

If you'd like to become more actively involved again, we always welcome volunteers in various capacities:

- **[Volunteering](/leaders/volunteering/)** - Become a registered leader
- **[Parent Roster](/leaders/parent-roster/)** - Support one meeting per term
- **[Adult Supporters](/leaders/adult-supporters/)** - Regular meeting attendance
- **[Committee](/committee/)** - Join our Group Committee

**[Contact Bardon Scouts](/contact/)**
