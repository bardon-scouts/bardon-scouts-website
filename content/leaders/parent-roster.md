Bardon Scouts is run by volunteers, so every family is asked to help. Through the parent roster, each family helps at **one Scout meeting per term**, and also helps with the group's fundraising during the year.

### Why We Have a Parent Roster

Extra adults keep supervision ratios where they need to be, so every Scout can join in safely. They also let leaders run more activity stations and give Scouts more individual attention. Sharing the load means no single volunteer is overburdened, and you get to see what your child is learning and meet other families.

### What Roster Duty Involves

At a meeting, you'll help set up and pack up, supervise an activity station, help leaders with games and challenges, and help with jobs like signing Scouts in and out. At fundraising events, you might help set up, run a stall, handle sales, or help clean up.

You don't need any Scouting experience. The leaders will tell you what's needed on the night.

### Booking Your Date

Your Section Leader organises the roster each term and will send you the available dates, what's planned, start and finish times, and any particular help needed. We'll work with you to find a date that suits.

If you can't make your rostered date, let your Section Leader know as soon as possible, and either swap with another family or arrange another date.

### Fundraising

Fundraising helps pay for our programs, equipment and the Den, and keeps costs down for every family. All families are expected to help. Examples include the [Mango Fundraiser](/mango-fundraiser/) (October to December), stalls at community events, working bees, and special events.

### Doing More

One meeting a term is the minimum. If you'd like to help more often, consider becoming an [Adult Supporter](/leaders/adult-supporters/), who helps at at least 50% of meetings, or see our other [volunteering opportunities](/leaders/volunteering/), including becoming a leader or joining the committee. You can also [meet our leaders](/leaders/our-leaders/).

For questions about the roster, speak to your Section Leader or [contact us](/contact/).
