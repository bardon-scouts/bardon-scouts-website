## Parent Roster

At Bardon Scouts, we believe that Scouting is a community effort. All parents are expected to contribute to the success of our programs through the parent roster system.

### What is the Parent Roster?

The parent roster ensures that we have adequate adult support at meetings and events throughout the year. This shared responsibility helps our volunteer leaders run safe, engaging programs for all our Scouts.

**Parent Roster Requirements:**
- **One meeting per term** - Each family is expected to attend and assist at one Scout meeting per term
- **Fundraising support** - Assistance with fundraising activities throughout the year

### Why Do We Have a Parent Roster?

**Safety and Supervision**
Additional adults help maintain appropriate supervision ratios, ensuring all Scouts can participate safely in activities.

**Program Quality**
With extra hands, leaders can run more engaging activities, provide individual attention to Scouts, and manage multiple activity stations.

**Community Building**
Parent involvement strengthens our Scout community, helps you understand what your child is learning, and allows you to meet other families.

**Fair Contribution**
Sharing the load ensures no single volunteer is overburdened and that all families contribute to our Scout group's success.

### What Does Roster Duty Involve?

**During Meetings:**
- Assisting with activity setup and pack-up
- Supervising activity stations
- Supporting leaders with games and challenges
- Helping with administrative tasks (sign-in/sign-out, etc.)
- General supervision and safety monitoring

**At Fundraising Events:**
- Helping with setup and pack-up
- Running stalls or activity stations
- Managing sales or collections
- Supporting event coordination
- Clean-up and logistics

### No Experience Required!

You don't need any special skills or Scouting knowledge to help on roster. Our experienced leaders will guide you and let you know what's needed. It's a great opportunity to see what your child does at Scouts and be part of their adventure.

### Scheduling Your Roster Duty

Your Section Leader will coordinate the parent roster for each term. You'll receive information about:
- Available dates for your roster duty
- What activities are planned (so you know what to expect)
- Arrival and finish times
- Any specific help needed

We understand families have busy schedules and will work with you to find suitable dates.

### Can't Make Your Rostered Date?

Life happens! If you can't make your rostered date:
- Contact your Section Leader as soon as possible
- Arrange to swap with another family
- Schedule an alternative date that works for you

### Beyond the Minimum

While one meeting per term is the minimum expectation, many parents find they enjoy helping out and volunteer more frequently. If you'd like to be more involved, consider becoming an [Adult Supporter](/leaders/adult-supporters/) or explore other [volunteering opportunities](/leaders/volunteering/).

### Fundraising Activities

Bardon Scouts runs several fundraising activities throughout the year to support our programs, equipment, and facilities. All families are expected to assist with these events.

**Examples of fundraising activities:**
- Mango fundraiser (October-December)
- Community event stalls and information booths
- Working bees and group activities
- Special events and celebrations

Your participation helps keep costs down for all families and ensures we can provide quality programs and equipment.

### Questions About Parent Roster?

If you have questions about the parent roster or need to discuss your roster commitments, please [contact us](/contact/) or speak with your child's Section Leader.

Remember: Your involvement makes a difference! Even one meeting per term helps our leaders provide better programs for all Scouts.
