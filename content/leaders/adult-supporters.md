To run Scout meetings effectively and provide quality programs, Bardon Scouts relies on Adult Supporters - parents willing to volunteer regularly to assist with meetings and activities.

### What is an Adult Supporter?

Adult Supporters are parents who commit to attending and assisting at a minimum of 50% of Scout meetings for their child's section. This regular involvement helps leaders deliver engaging, safe programs and creates a consistent, supportive environment for all Scouts.

**Commitment:**
- Attend at least 50% of weekly Scout meetings
- Assist with activities and supervision
- Support leaders with program delivery
- Help with setup and pack-up

### Why We Need Adult Supporters

**Effective Program Delivery**
With regular adult support, leaders can run more diverse activities, provide better supervision, and give Scouts more individual attention.

**Safety and Supervision**
Additional adults ensure we maintain appropriate supervision ratios for all activities, from outdoor adventures to craft projects.

**Consistency**
Regular Adult Supporters create a stable environment where Scouts feel supported and leaders can plan confidently knowing help will be available.

**Leadership Development**
Adult Supporters help demonstrate teamwork, community service, and positive role modeling for young people.

### What Makes Being an Adult Supporter Rewarding?

**Watch Your Child Grow**
See firsthand the skills, confidence, and friendships your child develops through Scouting. You'll have a front-row seat to their adventures and achievements.

**Meet Other Parents**
Build meaningful friendships with other parents in your section. Adult Supporters form a strong community of like-minded families who support each other beyond Scout meetings.

**Get Involved in Your Community**
Be an active part of your local community, contribute to youth development, and make a tangible difference in children's lives.

**Learn New Skills**
From outdoor skills to youth engagement techniques, you'll gain valuable experience and knowledge that benefits both you and your family.

**It's Fun!**
Scouting is about adventure, games, challenges, and laughter. Adult Supporters often say they have as much fun as the kids!

**Make Lasting Memories**
The experiences you share with your child and their Scout friends create memories that last a lifetime - for both you and them.

### What Does an Adult Supporter Do?

**During Meetings:**
- Help set up activity areas and equipment
- Supervise activity stations and games
- Assist Scouts with challenges and skill development
- Support leaders with program activities
- Monitor safety and behavior
- Help with pack-up and clean-up

**No Formal Leadership Required:**
Adult Supporters work under the guidance of qualified Scout Leaders. You'll assist with activities but won't be responsible for planning programs or leading the section.

**Flexibility Within Commitment:**
While we ask for 50% attendance minimum, we understand life happens. Coordinate with other Adult Supporters and your Section Leader when you can't make a meeting.

### No Experience Needed!

You don't need any Scouting experience or special skills to be an Adult Supporter. Our experienced leaders will guide you, and you'll learn as you go. Your enthusiasm and willingness to help are the most important qualities.

**What We Provide:**
- Guidance from experienced leaders
- Information about activities and what's needed
- A welcoming, supportive environment
- Training opportunities if you want to learn more

### Blue Card Requirement

As an Adult Supporter attending regularly, you'll need a Queensland Blue Card (Working with Children Check). This is a free process for volunteers, and we'll guide you through the application.

**Why Blue Card is Required:**
All adults working regularly with children in Queensland must hold a Blue Card to ensure child safety. This is a standard requirement across all youth organizations.

### Building Community

Adult Supporters form the heart of our Scout community. Beyond meetings, Adult Supporters often:
- Socialize with other parents and families
- Support each other's children at events
- Participate in parent social activities
- Build lasting friendships within the group
- Create a network of support for all families

Many of our closest friendships at Bardon Scouts are between Adult Supporter parents who started volunteering together and discovered a wonderful community.

### Different from Parent Roster

Adult Supporters go beyond the minimum [Parent Roster](/leaders/parent-roster/) requirement of one meeting per term. This regular commitment (50%+ of meetings) provides consistent support that makes a significant difference to program quality.

**Can't Commit to 50%?**
That's okay! Every family is still expected to participate in the [Parent Roster](/leaders/parent-roster/) (one meeting per term). The Adult Supporter role is for those who can commit to more regular involvement.

### Ready to Become an Adult Supporter?

If you're interested in becoming an Adult Supporter and making a meaningful contribution to your child's Scout section, we'd love to hear from you!

**[Contact us](/contact/)** or speak with your child's Section Leader to express your interest.

### Next Steps

1. **Express interest** - Contact your Section Leader or [contact us](/contact/)
2. **Attend a few meetings** - See what it's all about and meet the team
3. **Apply for your Blue Card** - We'll guide you through the free application process
4. **Start helping regularly** - Join the Adult Supporter team!

### Want to Do More?

If you find you love being an Adult Supporter and want even greater involvement, consider:
- **[Becoming a Scout Leader](/leaders/volunteering/)** - Take on a formal leadership role with training and support
- **[Joining the Committee](/committee/)** - Help with governance, fundraising, and group operations
- **Specialist support** - Share specific skills (first aid, IT, outdoor skills, etc.)
