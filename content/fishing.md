Fishing is a year-round activity at Bardon Scouts, open to every section.

## Event Details

**When:** Throughout the year (dates to be announced)
**Who:** Joeys, Cubs, Scouts, Venturers - all sections
**Where:** The fishing platform near Breakfast Creek (to be confirmed)

## Where We Fish

Our leaders plan to fish from the platform near Breakfast Creek, where catfish, bream and other fish that like brackish water are caught. Leaders are still checking the details.

For a bigger day out, we may also fish off the old Hornibrook Bridge at Sandgate.
