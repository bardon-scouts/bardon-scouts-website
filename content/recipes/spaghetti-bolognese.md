1. Heat the oil in the larger Trangia pot. Cook the onion and garlic for 3 minutes, stirring, until soft.
2. Add the mince and break it up with a wooden spoon. Cook until it's browned all over.
3. Stir in the carrot, tomatoes, tomato paste and herbs. Add half a can of water, bring to a simmer and cook with the lid on for 15 minutes, stirring now and then so it doesn't catch. Season with the salt and pepper.
4. Move the sauce into the smaller pot (or a billy) and keep it warm at the edge of the fire or wrapped in a tea towel.
5. Fill the larger pot two-thirds with water and bring it to the boil. Add the spaghetti and cook for 10 to 12 minutes, until tender.
6. Drain the spaghetti carefully, away from people, and share it between plates. Spoon the sauce over and top with the parmesan.
