Recipes our leaders use on camps and hikes. Tick the ones you want, or use **Add to cookbook** on a recipe's page, then open your cookbook to print it or save it as a PDF.

Dietary notes are a general guide only. Check the ingredients against the allergies and needs of everyone you're cooking for.
