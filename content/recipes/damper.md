1. Put the flour and salt in a large bowl. Rub the butter into the flour with your fingertips until it looks like breadcrumbs.
2. Make a well in the middle and pour in most of the milk. Mix with a knife until it comes together as a soft dough, adding the rest of the milk if it's dry.
3. Turn the dough out onto a floured board and knead it gently for a minute. Shape it into a round about 5 cm high.
4. **Camp oven:** dust the inside of a camp oven with flour, put the damper in and cut a cross in the top. Put the lid on, sit the oven on a few coals and shovel more coals onto the lid. Cook for 25 to 30 minutes.
5. **On sticks:** roll small pieces of dough into long snakes and wind them around clean green sticks. Hold them over the coals, turning, until golden and cooked through.
6. The damper is cooked when it sounds hollow when you tap the bottom. Serve warm with butter, golden syrup or jam.
