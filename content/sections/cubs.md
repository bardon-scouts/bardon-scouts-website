## Welcome to Cubs!

Cubs (ages 8-11) is where adventure begins! Our Cub pack meets every Tuesday evening for games, activities, and planning exciting adventures.

## What We Do

Cubs is all about learning through fun and adventure. Cubs participate in:

- **Outdoor Adventures** - Regular camps, hikes, and outdoor activities
- **Skill Development** - Learning practical skills like cooking, first aid, and navigation
- **Team Challenges** - Working together to solve problems and complete challenges
- **Community Service** - Participating in community events and helping others
- **Creative Activities** - Arts, crafts, drama, and music

## Our Program

The Cub program follows the Australian Scouts program, with activities designed specifically for 8-11 year olds. Cubs work towards achievement badges while having fun and making friends.

## Meeting Times

**When:** Tuesdays, 6:30 - 8:00pm
**Where:** Bardon Scout Den, 11 Bee Street, Bardon

## Join Cubs

If your child is 8-11 years old and ready for adventure, Cubs is the perfect place to start!
