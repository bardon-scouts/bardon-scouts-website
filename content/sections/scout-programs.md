These are the programs we use for Scout meetings, with what you need and how to run each one. They're here for leaders and adult supporters helping on the night. When we tell parents which program we're running, we link to it on this page.

- [Mt Coot-tha Hike](#mt-coot-tha-hike)

## Mt Coot-tha Hike

**Aim:** an evening hike up Mt Coot-tha, finishing with ice cream at the top.

**Start:** 6:30pm at the JC Slaughter Falls car park. Meet at the entrance on the right ([map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/E1gyfX2aaBobVdq27)).

**Finish:** 8:30pm at the Mount Coot-tha Summit Lookout, where we have ice cream and parents pick up ([map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/zh3ciMMcTNbHoyeK7)).

### What to bring

- Backpack
- Head torch
- $10 for ice cream
- Personal first aid kit
- Water bottle
- Jumper in winter
- Shoes for walking (no thongs or sandals)
