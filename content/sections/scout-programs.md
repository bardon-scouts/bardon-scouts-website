## Mt Coot-tha Hike

**Aim:** an evening hike up Mt Coot-tha, finishing with ice cream at the top.

**Start:** 6:30pm at the JC Slaughter Falls car park. Meet at the entrance on the right ([map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/E1gyfX2aaBobVdq27)).

**Finish:** 8:30pm at the Mount Coot-tha Summit Lookout, where we have ice cream and parents pick up ([map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/zh3ciMMcTNbHoyeK7)).

### What to bring

- Backpack
- Head torch
- $10 for ice cream
- Personal first aid kit
- Water bottle
- Jumper in winter
- Shoes for walking (no thongs or sandals)

## Trading Post

**Aim:** for Scouts to work in patrols, earning small amounts for small jobs and building up to larger amounts. The patrol with the largest amount wins and chooses from the prizes on offer.

**Setup:** the adults set up a set of tables that act as a Trading Post, where Scouts can buy and sell equipment. A board lists the prices of what they can buy and sell.

### How to qualify

Scouts must qualify by doing both of these:

- Make a fire
- Build a structure (tripod, A-frame or similar)
