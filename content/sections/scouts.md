## Welcome to Scouts!

Scouts (ages 11-15) is where the real adventure begins! Our Scout Troop meets every Thursday evening for activities that challenge, inspire, and excite.

## What We Do

Scouts take the lead in planning and running their own adventures. Activities include:

- **Adventure Activities** - Camping, hiking, abseiling, canoeing, and more
- **Leadership Development** - Taking responsibility and leading activities
- **Outdoor Skills** - Navigation, bushcraft, survival skills, and outdoor cooking
- **Community Engagement** - ANZAC Day services, community projects, fundraising
- **Special Interest Activities** - From technology to sports to environmental conservation

## Our Program

The Scout program is youth-led, with Scouts making decisions about their activities and adventures. Scouts work towards challenging badges and participate in regional and national events.

## Meeting Times

**When:** Thursdays, 6:30 - 8:30pm
**Where:** Bardon Scout Den, 11 Bee Street, Bardon
