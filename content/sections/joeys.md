## Welcome to Joeys!

Joeys (ages 6-8) is where the Scouting adventure begins! Our Joey mob meets every Monday evening for fun, games, and learning through play.

## What We Do

Joeys is all about learning through play and friendship. Joey Scouts participate in:

- **Fun and Games** - Active play, indoor and outdoor games
- **Creative Activities** - Arts, crafts, storytelling, and imaginative play
- **Basic Skills** - Introduction to outdoor skills in a fun, age-appropriate way
- **Making Friends** - Building friendships and learning to work as a team
- **Community Awareness** - Learning about helping others and our community

## Our Program

The Joey program follows the Australian Scouts program, with activities designed specifically for 6-8 year olds. Joeys explore, discover, and have fun in a safe and supportive environment.

## Meeting Times

**When:** Mondays, 6:00 - 7:00pm
**Where:** Bardon Scout Den, 11 Bee Street, Bardon

## Join Joeys

If your child is 6-8 years old and ready to start their Scouting journey, Joeys is the perfect place to begin!
