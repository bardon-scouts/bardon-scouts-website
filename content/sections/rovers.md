## Rovers (18-25 Years) - Coming Soon!

We are planning to establish a Rover Scout section at Bardon Scouts for young adults aged 18 to 25 years.

### What is Rovers?

Rover Scouts is the final and most senior section of Scouting, designed for young adults who want to continue their Scouting journey through adventure, service, and personal development.

**Key Features of Rovers:**
- Young adult-led program focusing on personal growth and community service
- Adventure activities including hiking, camping, and international travel
- Service projects benefiting the local community and broader Scouting movement
- Leadership opportunities mentoring younger Scouts
- Social events and fellowship with other young adults
- Flexible involvement around work, study, and personal commitments

### Why Join Rovers?

**Continue Your Scouting Adventure**
If you've been a Scout and want to continue the journey, Rovers offers new challenges and opportunities suited to young adults.

**Start Fresh**
Never been a Scout? Rovers welcomes new members! You don't need prior Scouting experience to join.

**Make a Difference**
Rovers are actively involved in community service, environmental projects, and supporting younger Scout sections.

**Build Lifelong Friendships**
Connect with like-minded young adults who share your interest in adventure, service, and personal development.

### About Rover Scouts in Queensland

While we establish our own Rover crew, you can learn more about Rovers through Scouts Queensland:

- **Age Range:** 18 to 25 years
- **Meeting Style:** Flexible, crew-determined schedule
- **Program Focus:** Adventure, service, personal development, leadership
- **Activities:** Determined by the crew based on interests and goals
