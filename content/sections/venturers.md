## Welcome to Venturers!

Venturers (ages 14-18) is all about big adventures, personal challenge, and preparing for life beyond school. Our Venturer Unit meets every Thursday evening.

## What We Do

Venturers are fully youth-led, planning and running their own program with adult support. Activities include:

- **Major Expeditions** - Multi-day hikes, bike tours, interstate and international trips
- **Adventure Sports** - Rock climbing, kayaking, caving, snow sports
- **Leadership Projects** - Planning and leading activities for younger sections
- **Community Leadership** - Service projects, advocacy, and making a difference
- **Life Skills** - First aid, outdoor survival, project management, and more

## Our Program

The Venturer program is completely youth-led. Venturers decide what they want to do, plan the details, and make it happen. This develops real-world leadership, teamwork, and project management skills.

## Meeting Times

**When:** Thursdays, 7:00 - 9:00pm
**Where:** Bardon Scout Den, 11 Bee Street, Bardon
