## Code of Conduct

Bardon Scouts is built on a foundation of respect, responsibility, and community. Our Code of Conduct helps ensure a safe, positive, and enjoyable environment for all members, families, and volunteers.

### For Parents and Adults

**Respect and Courtesy**

- Treat all Scouts, parents, leaders, and volunteers with respect and courtesy
- Model positive behavior and language for young people
- Support leaders in maintaining discipline and program standards
- Respect the decisions and guidance of Scout Leaders

**Attendance and Communication**

- **[Sign In and Out](/sign-in-out/)** - Always sign your child in and out at every meeting and event
- Notify your Section Leader if your child will be absent from a meeting or event
- **Respond to RSVPs** - Reply to Consent2go permission requests promptly, even if your child won't be attending
- Inform leaders of any changes to your child's medical information or emergency contacts

**Parent Roster Commitment**

- **[Parent Roster](/leaders/parent-roster/)** - Fulfill your commitment to assist with one meeting per term
- Support fundraising activities and group events
- Coordinate with other parents if you cannot make your scheduled roster duty

**Property and Facilities**

- Respect the Scout Hall and all equipment
- Supervise your children before and after meetings to prevent damage to property
- Leave all areas clean and tidy

**Privacy and Safety**

- **No Tracking Devices** - Do not use AirTags or other tracking devices on your child during Scout activities. Scouts are supervised by Leaders, and tracking devices compromise the privacy of other Scouts and families
- Respect the privacy of other families and Scouts
- Follow all safety guidelines and instructions from Leaders

**Language and Behavior**

- Use appropriate language at all times - no swearing or offensive language
- Address any concerns or issues privately with Section Leaders or Group Leaders, not in front of Scouts
- Support a positive, inclusive environment for all

**Phone Use**

- **No phone use during Scout meetings** - Parents attending roster duty or as Adult Supporters should not use phones before, during, or after meetings unless for emergencies. See our **[Phone Use](/phone-use/)** policy for details.

### For Scouts

**The Scout Promise and Law**

As Scouts, you have made a promise to do your best. Living our Code of Conduct is part of keeping that promise.

**Respect for Everyone**

- Treat all Scouts, leaders, and parents with respect and kindness
- Listen when leaders or other Scouts are speaking
- Include everyone in activities - leave no one out
- Respect different backgrounds, abilities, and opinions

**Respect for Property**

- Take care of the Scout Hall, equipment, and gear
- Use equipment properly and safely
- Clean up after activities and leave areas tidy
- Report any damage to leaders immediately

**Language and Behavior**

- Use appropriate language - no swearing or offensive words
- Follow the Scout Law in your words and actions
- Help create a positive environment for everyone

**Attendance and Participation**

- Attend meetings regularly and on time
- Let your parents know if you'll be absent so they can inform leaders
- Participate actively in activities and challenges
- Do your best in everything you try

**Safety**

- Follow all safety instructions from leaders
- Stay with your patrol or group during activities
- Report any injuries or problems to leaders immediately
- Look out for the safety of yourself and others

**Phone Use**

- **No phones during Scout meetings** - Phones should not be used before, during, or after meetings. See our **[Phone Use](/phone-use/)** policy for full details.
- Emergency contact is available through leaders if needed

### Consequences of Code Violations

Our Code of Conduct exists to keep everyone safe and ensure a positive experience for all. Violations of this code may result in:

- Discussion with the Scout and parents
- Temporary suspension from activities
- In serious or repeated cases, removal from the Scout Group

We prefer to work with families to address concerns positively and help Scouts learn from mistakes.

### Youth Protection

Bardon Scouts is committed to providing a safe environment for all young people. All leaders and volunteers:

- Hold current Queensland Blue Cards (Working with Children Check)
- Complete youth protection training
- Follow Scouts Queensland child safety standards
- Maintain appropriate supervision ratios at all activities

### Questions or Concerns?

If you have questions about our Code of Conduct or wish to discuss a concern, please [contact us](/contact/) or speak with your Section Leader or Group Leader confidentially.
