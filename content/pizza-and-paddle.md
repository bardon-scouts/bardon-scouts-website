## Adventure on the Water

Pizza and Paddle is a fun Friday night activity at Brownsea Water Activities Centre where Scouts paddle canoes on the water and enjoy pizza under the stars. It's a great way to do something different, practice water skills, and meet other Scouts from across the region!

## Event Details

**When:** Friday nights (various dates throughout the year)
**Time:** 6:45pm - 10:00pm
**Where:** [Brownsea Water Activities Centre](https://brownsea.qldscouts.com/)

**Check the** [Brownsea Calendar](https://brownsea.qldscouts.com/?page_id=215) **for upcoming Pizza and Paddle dates**

## About Brownsea Water Activities Centre

Brownsea is Queensland Scouts' premier water activities centre, offering sailing, kayaking, and canoeing programs for Scouts from Northern NSW to Bundaberg. Located on the water, Brownsea provides the perfect setting for Scouts to develop water skills, build confidence, and experience outdoor adventure.

The centre offers:
- Canoe and kayak activities
- Sailing programs
- Power boat licensing
- Camping and bunkhouse accommodation
- Specialist water activities programs

[Visit Brownsea Water Activities Centre →](https://brownsea.qldscouts.com/)

## What Happens at Pizza and Paddle?

Pizza and Paddle Nights combine canoeing with a social pizza dinner, making for an exciting and memorable evening on the water.

**Event Schedule:**
- **6:45pm** - Registration and arrival
- **7:00pm** - Safety briefing
- **7:10pm** - Canoe preparation
- **7:30pm** - Paddling begins
- **9:00pm** - Return to shore and cleanup
- **9:30pm** - Pizza served under the stars
- **9:50pm** - Pack up and cleanup
- **10:00pm** - Event concludes

**Activities Include:**
- Canoeing on the water at sunset
- Practicing paddling skills and teamwork
- Meeting Scouts from other groups
- Enjoying pizza dinner outdoors
- Social time with fellow Scouts

## Parent Information

### Who can go?
**Scouts only** (ages 11-15 years)

Pizza and Paddle is specifically for Scouts. Participants must have been invested at least as a Scout. Cubs, Joeys, and Venturers have separate programs at Brownsea.

*Note: Venturers and Rovers may also attend Pizza and Paddle events*

### What to bring:
- **Change of clothes** (you will get wet!)
- **Towel**
- **Warm layer** for after paddling
- **Water bottle**
- **Closed-toe shoes** that can get wet (no thongs/flip-flops)
- **Hat and sunscreen** (if still daylight when paddling)
- **Personal flotation device (PFD)** if you have one (otherwise provided)

### Important Requirements:
- **Must be a swimmer** - All participants must be able to swim
- **Current Scout membership** - Must be a financial member for insurance coverage
- **Arrive on time** - Registration starts at 6:45pm
- **Parent pickup** - Parents pick up from Brownsea at 10:00pm

## Travel Arrangements

### Drop-off: Roma Street Train Station
Parents drop Scouts at **Roma Street Train Station** where the group will meet leaders and travel together to Brownsea Water Activities Centre.

**Drop-off Details:**
- Meeting point will be confirmed by your Section Leader
- Arrive on time for group departure
- Scouts travel together under leader supervision

### Pick-up: Brownsea Water Activities Centre
Parents collect Scouts from **Brownsea Water Activities Centre** at the conclusion of the event.

**Pick-up Details:**
- **Time:** 10:00pm
- **Location:** Brownsea Water Activities Centre (address provided by leaders)
- Please be punctual for collection
- Scouts will be supervised until all are collected

*Specific travel details, meeting times, and addresses will be provided by your Section Leader prior to the event*

## About Brownsea Water Activities

Brownsea has been providing water-based Scouting activities for decades, offering Scouts the opportunity to develop skills, build confidence, and experience adventure on the water.

Programs at Brownsea include:
- **Joey Fun Days** - Introductory water activities for Joeys
- **Cub Days** - Canoe and pirate ship activities for Cubs
- **Pizza and Paddle** - Evening canoe sessions for Scouts
- **Paddling Days** - Full-day water activities programs
- **Power Boat Licensing** - Certification courses
- **Sailing Programs** - Learn to sail activities

The centre serves Scouts throughout Southeast Queensland and Northern NSW, making it a regional hub for water-based Scouting.

## Safety & Requirements

Brownsea Water Activities Centre maintains high safety standards for all programs:

✓ **Qualified Instructors** - All activities led by trained water activities leaders
✓ **Safety Equipment** - PFDs (life jackets) and safety equipment provided
✓ **Safety Briefings** - Comprehensive briefing before water activities
✓ **Supervision** - Appropriate adult-to-youth ratios maintained
✓ **Emergency Procedures** - First aid and emergency response plans in place

**Swimming Requirement:**
All participants must be swimmers. This is a safety requirement and will be confirmed during registration.

**Insurance:**
Participants must be current, financial Scout members for insurance coverage.
