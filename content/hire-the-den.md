When the Bardon Scout Group Den isn't being used for Scout meetings and events, it may be available to hire.

### Where it is

The Den is at 11 Bee Street, Bardon. Parking is limited to Bee Street and the surrounding streets.

### How to enquire

Fill in the form below with the dates you'd like. We'll let you know if the Den is available, and tell you about the facilities, what it can be used for, the conditions of hire (such as insurance and cleaning up afterwards) and the cost.
