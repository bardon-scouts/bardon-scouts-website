## District Swimming Competition

District Swimming is an exciting competition where Cub Scouts from Bardon compete against Cubs from neighboring Scout Groups in swimming races. This friendly competition brings together our district—Bardon, Oakleigh, The Gap, and St Johns Wood—for a day of aquatic challenges, team spirit, and sporting fun.

## Event Details

**When:** Annually (date announced by district)
**Duration:** Typically a half-day or full-day event
**Where:** Local swimming pool facility
**Who:** Cub Scouts (8-11 years) only

## About District Swimming

District Swimming is a competitive swimming gala organized at the district level, giving Cub Scouts the opportunity to represent their group in friendly races against Cubs from neighboring Scout Groups. The event combines individual swimming races with team competitions, all in the spirit of Scouting fun and camaraderie.

**Our District Competitors:**
Cubs from four groups compete together:
- **Bardon Scouts** - Our home group
- **Oakleigh Scout Group**
- **The Gap Scout Group**
- **St Johns Wood Scout Group**

**What Makes District Swimming Special:**
- **Competitive Racing** - Individual and relay races
- **Team Spirit** - Represent Bardon Scouts and cheer for teammates
- **District Pride** - Compete alongside Cubs from neighboring groups
- **Swimming Skills** - Showcase abilities learned in swimming lessons
- **Age Categories** - Fair competition within age groups
- **Multiple Strokes** - Race in various swimming styles
- **Fun & Friendship** - Enjoy friendly competition and Scouting spirit
- **Awards & Recognition** - Ribbons, certificates, or trophies for winners

## Swimming Events

District Swimming typically includes a variety of race formats and distances:

**Individual Races:**
- **Freestyle** - Front crawl races (various distances)
- **Backstroke** - Backstroke races (various distances)
- **Breaststroke** - Breaststroke races (where applicable)
- **Width Races** - Shorter races for younger or less experienced swimmers
- **Length Races** - 25-metre or 50-metre races for stronger swimmers

**Team Events:**
- **Relay Races** - Teams of 4 Cubs racing together
- **Freestyle Relays** - Fast-paced team competition
- **Mixed Stroke Relays** - Each swimmer uses a different stroke

**Age Categories:**
Races are typically divided into age groups to ensure fair competition:
- 8-9 years
- 10-11 years
- Or other age divisions determined by the district

**Ability Levels:**
Events may be organized to accommodate different swimming abilities, ensuring all Cubs can participate regardless of skill level.

## Event Format

**Typical District Swimming Day:**

**Morning/Arrival**
- **Check-in** - Register and receive race numbers
- **Warm-up** - Supervised warm-up swim time
- **Opening Ceremony** - Welcome and event overview
- **Team Assembly** - Groups organize by sections and age categories

**Competition**
- **Individual Races** - Cubs compete in their designated events
- **Relay Races** - Team competitions
- **Heats & Finals** - Some events may have heats with finals for top swimmers
- **Cheering Section** - Teammates cheer from the sidelines

**Closing**
- **Final Races** - Complete remaining events
- **Awards Ceremony** - Recognition for race winners and team spirit
- **Closing Remarks** - Thank competitors and volunteers
- **Departure** - Pack up and head home

*Exact format varies by district organization and pool facility*

## Parent Information

### Who can go?
**Cub Scouts (8-11 years) only**

District Swimming is specifically for the Cub Scout section. Cubs compete in age-appropriate race categories based on their swimming ability.

### Swimming Requirements:
- **Must be able to swim** - Cubs should be comfortable swimming independently
- **Swimming ability varies** - Events designed for different skill levels
- **No minimum distance required** - Some events are shorter for developing swimmers
- **Safety first** - Lifeguards and supervision throughout the event

### What to bring:
- **Swimsuit** (appropriate for racing)
- **Towel** (for after swimming)
- **Goggles** (if your Cub uses them)
- **Swim cap** (if required or preferred)
- **Change of clothes** (warm, dry clothes for after swimming)
- **Water bottle** (stay hydrated)
- **Snacks or lunch** (depending on event duration)
- **Scout uniform** (may be worn before/after for opening/closing ceremonies)
- **Sunscreen** (if outdoor pool)
- **Warm layer** (for between races)
- **Plastic bag** (for wet swimsuit)

### Important Information:
- **Registration** - Sign up through your Cub Leader
- **Deadline** - Register before the cut-off to secure your spot
- **Age verification** - Age categories based on date of birth
- **Events selected** - Choose which races your Cub will enter during registration
- **Arrival time** - Arrive early for check-in and warm-up
- **Supervision** - Cubs remain with Bardon Cub Leaders throughout event
- **Parent spectators** - Families welcome to attend and cheer!

### Travel & Logistics:
- **Venue** - Swimming pool location confirmed before event
- **Drop-off/Pick-up** - Times provided by Cub Leaders
- **Group travel** - Bardon Cubs may travel together or meet at venue
- **Parking** - Information provided for venue parking

## Safety & Supervision

District Swimming events maintain comprehensive safety:

✓ **Qualified Lifeguards** - On duty throughout the event
✓ **Leader Supervision** - Cub Leaders from all groups present
✓ **Pool Safety** - Racing in controlled pool environment
✓ **Age-Appropriate** - Races designed for Cub Scout abilities
✓ **First Aid** - First aid trained leaders and kits available
✓ **Warm-Up Time** - Supervised warm-up before competition
✓ **Rest Periods** - Breaks between races to prevent fatigue
✓ **Hydration Stations** - Water available throughout event
✓ **Emergency Procedures** - Clear protocols in place

**Swimming Ability:**
Cubs compete in events appropriate to their swimming ability. Events are structured to ensure all participants can swim safely in their designated races.

## Registration & Participation

**How to Register:**

- Cubs select which events to enter (freestyle, backstroke, relays, etc.)

- Relay team members selected closer to event
