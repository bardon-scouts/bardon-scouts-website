## Camping with Our District

District Camps bring together Scout Groups from across our local district for weekend camping adventures. Bardon Scouts joins forces with our neighboring groups—Oakleigh, The Gap, and St Johns Wood—for fun-filled weekends of outdoor activities, friendship, and shared Scouting experiences.

## Event Details

**When:** Throughout the year (dates vary by section and district calendar)
**Duration:** Typically weekend camps (Friday evening to Sunday)
**Where:** Various Scout campsites across Southeast Queensland
**Who:** Joey Scouts (6-8 years), Cub Scouts (8-11 years), and Scouts (11-15 years)

## About District Camps

District Camps are organized at the district level, bringing together multiple Scout Groups for larger-scale camping experiences. These camps allow Bardon Scouts to connect with Scouts from neighboring groups while enjoying outdoor adventures in a supportive, community environment.

**Our District Groups:**
Bardon Scouts camps with fellow groups in our district:
- **Bardon Scouts** - Our home group
- **Oakleigh Scout Group**
- **The Gap Scout Group**
- **St Johns Wood Scout Group**

**What Makes District Camps Special:**
- **Larger Community** - Meet Scouts from neighboring groups
- **Shared Activities** - Enjoy activities designed for combined groups
- **District Spirit** - Build connections across the wider Scouting community
- **Section-Specific** - Camps tailored for Joeys, Cubs, or Scouts
- **Outdoor Adventure** - Weekend camping at quality Scout campsites
- **New Friendships** - Form bonds with Scouts beyond your own group
- **District Events** - Participate in district-wide challenges and competitions

## Section-Specific Camps

District Camps are organized by section, ensuring age-appropriate activities and supervision.

### Joey Scout District Camps

**Ages:** 6-8 years

Joey District Camps provide young Scouts with their first multi-group camping experience:
- Simple camping activities and outdoor fun
- Age-appropriate challenges and games
- Meeting Joeys from other groups in the district
- Shorter duration camps perfect for first-time campers
- Adult leader supervision from all participating groups

**Typical Activities:**
- Campfire songs and stories
- Nature walks and exploration
- Craft activities
- Wide games and team challenges
- Outdoor cooking experiences

### Cub Scout District Camps

**Ages:** 8-11 years

Cub District Camps offer more adventurous activities for this age group:
- Extended camping experiences
- Skill development activities
- Inter-group games and competitions
- Badge work and Milestone progress
- Leadership opportunities within patrols

**Typical Activities:**
- Camping skills and outdoor cooking
- Navigation and orienteering
- Water activities (where available)
- Rope work and pioneering
- Sports and team games
- Environmental activities
- Campfire programs

### Scout District Camps

**Ages:** 11-15 years

Scout District Camps provide challenging outdoor experiences:
- Advanced camping and bushcraft skills
- Leadership development activities
- District-wide competitions
- Outdoor Adventure Skills progression
- Patrol-based challenges

**Typical Activities:**
- Advanced navigation and hiking
- Pioneering projects
- Outdoor cooking competitions
- Wide games and night activities
- Skill-based challenges
- Inter-patrol competitions
- Environmental conservation projects

## Typical District Camp Format

**Friday Evening**
- **Arrival** - Groups arrive at campsite and set up camp
- **Opening Ceremony** - Welcome from district leaders
- **Ice Breakers** - Activities to help groups meet each other
- **Campfire** - Songs, stories, and entertainment
- **Lights Out** - Sleep in tents by section

**Saturday**
- **Morning** - Breakfast and camp duties
- **Morning Activities** - Skill stations, challenges, or activities
- **Lunch** - Outdoor cooking by patrols or provided meals
- **Afternoon Activities** - Wide games, sports, or adventure activities
- **Free Time** - Rest, games, or section activities
- **Evening Meal** - Dinner prepared by patrols or leaders
- **Evening Program** - Campfire, entertainment, or night activities
- **Lights Out** - Settle in for the night

**Sunday**
- **Morning** - Breakfast and pack up tents
- **Morning Activity** - Final challenge or activity
- **Closing Ceremony** - Awards, recognition, and farewell
- **Departure** - Head home with memories

*Schedule varies by section and specific camp program*

## Parent Information

### Who can go?
**Joey Scouts (6-8 years), Cub Scouts (8-11 years), and Scouts (11-15 years)**

District Camps are organized by section, so your child will camp with their age group. Each section has separate camps throughout the year.

### What to bring:
- **Tent** (if required—sometimes patrols share tents)
- **Sleeping bag** (rated for season)
- **Sleeping mat** or mattress
- **Backpack or bag** for gear
- **Clothes** for the weekend (layers for changing weather)
- **Rain jacket and warm layers**
- **Sturdy shoes or boots** (closed-toe, broken in)
- **Hat and sunscreen**
- **Water bottle** (1-2 litres)
- **Plate, cup, cutlery** (for meals)
- **Torch/headlamp** with spare batteries
- **Toiletries** (toothbrush, soap, towel)
- **Personal medications** (clearly labeled)
- **Scout uniform** (for opening/closing ceremonies)
- **Warm clothes for sleeping**

**Section Leaders provide a complete packing list before each camp**

### Important Requirements:
- **Current Scout membership** - Financial members for insurance coverage
- **Completed forms** - Permission forms and medical information
- **Dietary needs** - Advise leaders of any allergies or dietary requirements
- **Medications** - Provide to leaders with clear instructions
- **Emergency contacts** - Keep contact details current

### Travel Arrangements:
- **Drop-off and pick-up** times and locations confirmed by leaders
- **Group travel** - Bardon Scouts typically travels together
- **Transport** - Arranged by Section Leaders (may include parent carpools)

## Campsites

District Camps are held at various Scout campsites across Southeast Queensland, chosen for their facilities and activities:

**Typical Campsite Features:**
- Tent camping areas
- Toilet and washing facilities
- Kitchen or cooking areas
- Activity spaces (ovals, bushland, water access)
- Campfire circles
- Shelter buildings
- Emergency vehicle access

**Past District Camp Locations:**
Locations vary by availability and district scheduling. Campsites are selected to provide safe, quality outdoor experiences for each section.

## Safety & Supervision

District Camps maintain high safety standards:

✓ **Trained Leaders** - Adult leaders from all participating groups
✓ **Appropriate Ratios** - Adequate adult-to-youth supervision maintained
✓ **Safety Briefings** - All participants receive safety instructions
✓ **First Aid** - First aid trained leaders and kits on site
✓ **Emergency Procedures** - Clear protocols and communication systems
✓ **Campsite Safety** - Sites selected for safety and suitability
✓ **Activity Risk Assessments** - All activities assessed for hazards
✓ **Medical Support** - Leaders informed of medical needs and allergies
✓ **Parent Contact** - Emergency contact details available

**District Coordination:**
District camps are coordinated by district leaders who ensure all groups follow Scouts Queensland policies and maintain consistent safety standards.

## Registration & Costs

**How to Participate:**
- District camp dates announced by Section Leaders
- Registration typically through your Section Leader
- Limited capacity based on campsite facilities
- Register early to secure your spot

**Cost:**
- Camp fees vary by location and duration
- Typically covers campsite fees, meals, and activity materials
- Cost details provided when registration opens
- Payment usually due before the camp

**What's Included:**
- Campsite facilities for the weekend
- Meals (or ingredients for patrol cooking)
- Activities and program materials
- Supervision and leadership throughout
- Insurance through Scouts Queensland membership
