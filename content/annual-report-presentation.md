## Our Annual General Meeting

The Annual Report Presentation (ARP) is Bardon Scouts' annual general meeting where we come together as a community to review the past year, celebrate achievements, examine our finances, and plan for the future. This important evening provides transparency, accountability, and an opportunity for all members of the Bardon Scouts community to be informed and involved in our group's direction.

## Event Details

**When:** Annually in March
**Time:** Typically 7:00pm-9:00pm (confirmed before event)
**Where:** Bardon Scouts Hall or designated venue
**Who:** All welcome - Parents, Leaders, Committee Members, Supporters

## About the Annual Report Presentation

The ARP is the formal annual meeting of Bardon Scouts Group, similar to an AGM (Annual General Meeting) for community organizations. This essential gathering brings together parents, leaders, committee members, and supporters to review the group's performance, finances, and activities over the past year.

**Purpose of the ARP:**
- Review the previous year's achievements and activities
- Present financial reports and budget
- Report on equipment and facilities
- Introduce committee members and elect new positions
- Hear from each section about their year's progress
- Plan for the upcoming year
- Recognize volunteers and contributors
- Provide transparency and accountability

## What Happens at the ARP

**Typical Agenda:**

**Welcome & Opening**
- Welcome from Group Leader or Chairperson
- Acknowledgment of attendees and apologies
- Adoption of previous year's minutes

**Reports & Presentations:**

**Financial Report**
- Treasurer presents annual financial statement
- Review of income and expenditure
- Budget overview for upcoming year
- Questions and discussion

**Equipment & Facilities Report**
- Status of Bardon Scouts Hall and facilities
- Equipment inventory and condition
- Maintenance completed and required
- Capital improvements and projects

**Committee Report**
- Introduction of current committee members
- Overview of committee activities and decisions
- Recognition of outgoing committee members
- Election of new committee positions

**Section Reports**
Each section presents their year in review:
- **Joey Scouts** - Achievements, highlights, and member growth
- **Cub Scouts** - Activities, camps, and skill development
- **Scouts** - Adventures, leadership, and progression
- **Venturers** - Challenges, projects, and achievements

**Group Leader's Report**
- Overview of the group's year
- Significant achievements and milestones
- Challenges faced and overcome
- Vision and goals for the upcoming year

**Recognition & Thanks**
- Volunteer appreciation
- Special recognition for outstanding contributions
- Thank you to supporters and sponsors

**Future Planning**
- Upcoming events and activities
- New initiatives and programs
- Goals for the year ahead
- Questions and input from attendees

**Closing**
- Final questions and discussion
- Thank you and adjournment

## Why the ARP Matters

**Transparency**
The ARP provides full transparency about how Bardon Scouts operates, including financial management and decision-making processes.

**Accountability**
Leaders and committee members report on their stewardship of the group, ensuring accountability to families and the community.

**Community Engagement**
All families have the opportunity to be informed, ask questions, and contribute to the group's direction.

**Recognition**
The ARP is a time to formally recognize and thank the many volunteers who make Bardon Scouts possible.

**Democratic Process**
Members participate in electing committee positions and approving important decisions.

**Planning & Vision**
The community comes together to discuss and shape the future direction of Bardon Scouts.

## Who Should Attend

**All families are welcome and encouraged to attend:**

**Parents & Guardians**
- Learn about your child's section activities
- Understand financial management
- Meet leaders and committee members
- Ask questions and provide input
- Participate in elections

**Leaders & Volunteers**
- Present section reports
- Support committee presentations
- Connect with families
- Plan for the upcoming year

**Committee Members**
- Present reports
- Stand for election or re-election
- Fulfill governance responsibilities

**Supporters & Community Members**
- Learn about Bardon Scouts' impact
- Support the organization
- Consider volunteering

## What to Expect

**Duration:** Approximately 2 hours

**Format:** Formal meeting with presentations, followed by Q&A and discussion

**Refreshments:** Light refreshments typically provided

**Dress:** Casual - come as you are

**Questions:** Opportunities for questions throughout and at the end

**Voting:** If required, voting procedures explained on the night

## Committee Elections

The ARP includes elections for committee positions. Positions may include:

**Executive Positions:**
- Group Leader
- Chairperson
- Secretary
- Treasurer

**Committee Positions:**
- Section Leaders (Joey, Cub, Scout, Venturer)
- Equipment Officer
- Fundraising Coordinator
- Communications Officer
- Other roles as needed

**Nominations:**
- Call for nominations typically issued before the ARP
- Nominations may be accepted from the floor
- Self-nomination or nomination by another member
- Nominees must consent to nomination

**Voting:**
- Democratic election process
- All financial members eligible to vote
- Voting procedures explained during meeting

## Financial Overview

The Treasurer's report provides a comprehensive financial overview:

**Income Sources:**
- Membership fees
- Fundraising activities (e.g., Mango Fundraiser)
- Grants and donations
- Activity fees
- Other revenue

**Expenditure Categories:**
- Hall maintenance and utilities
- Equipment and supplies
- Scout activities and camps
- Insurance and registrations
- Administration costs
- Capital improvements

**Financial Position:**
- Assets and liabilities
- Bank balances
- Outstanding commitments
- Financial health indicators

- Goals and initiatives implemented
- Community feedback incorporated
- Continuous improvement focus
