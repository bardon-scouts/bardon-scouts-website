This page explains what personal information the Bardon Scouts website collects through its forms, and what we do with it. Bardon Scout Group is part of Scouts Queensland, and the [Scouts Queensland Privacy Policy](https://scoutsqld.com.au/privacy-policy/) applies to everything we do, including this website. It covers members' records, Consent2go and anything else not described here.

## What the forms collect

The website only collects the details you type into one of its forms:

| Form | What it asks for |
|---|---|
| [Contact](/contact/) | Your name, email and message |
| [Ask to Join](/ask-to-join/) | Your name and email, your children's names and ages, and an optional message |
| [Website Feedback](/website-feedback/) | Your name, an optional email, the page, the kind of feedback and the details |
| [Complaints](/complaints/) | Your name, email, an optional phone number, the kind of complaint, when it happened, the details and how you'd like us to reply |
| [Hire the Den](/hire-the-den/) | Your name, organisation, email, an optional phone number, the dates and any details |

The forms never ask for payment details, bank details or passwords. Please don't include them, or other sensitive information, in a message.

## How we use it

We use what you send only to reply to you and deal with what you asked about: answering a question, arranging a trial meeting, fixing the website, handling a complaint or a Den hire. Only the leaders and committee members who handle that request see it. We don't sell your details, add you to a mailing list, or share them outside Scouts unless the law requires it.

## How it's stored

The website is hosted by Netlify. When you send a form, Netlify stores the submission and emails it to a Bardon Scouts group mailbox, not to a leader's personal email. Netlify may store it on servers outside Australia.

The website doesn't use analytics or advertising cookies, and doesn't track you from page to page.

## Seeing, correcting or removing your details

To ask what we hold about you, correct it or have it deleted, or to raise a privacy concern, use the [contact form](/contact/) or the [complaints form](/complaints/).
