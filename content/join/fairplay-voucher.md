The Queensland Government's FairPlay voucher program helps families with the cost of getting kids active in sports and recreation activities, including Scouting.

### What is the FairPlay Voucher?

FairPlay vouchers are $200 vouchers provided by the Queensland Government to help cover the cost of membership, registration, and participation fees for eligible sports and recreation activities.

**Key Details:**
- **Voucher Value:** $200 per child
- **Eligibility:** All Queensland children aged 5 to 17 years
- **Limit:** One voucher per child per financial year
- **Valid Until:** June 2025 (for current round)

### How to Use Your Voucher with Bardon Scouts

FairPlay vouchers can be used towards your Scouts Queensland membership fees at Bardon Scouts.

**To redeem your voucher:**
1. Apply for your FairPlay voucher through the Queensland Government website
2. Once approved, forward your voucher to our Group Treasurer
3. The voucher will be processed through Scouts Queensland Finance Team
4. The $200 will be credited towards your membership fees

### How to Apply for a FairPlay Voucher

**Apply Online:**
Visit the Queensland Government FairPlay website to check eligibility and apply:

**[Apply for FairPlay Voucher](https://www.qld.gov.au/recreation/sports/funding/fairplay)**

**Information You'll Need:**
- Your child's details (name, date of birth)
- Proof of Queensland residency
- Details about the activity (Scouts Queensland)

### About the FairPlay Program

The FairPlay voucher program has been expanded to help more Queensland families get their kids active and involved in sports and recreation. The program now offers up to 200,000 vouchers annually to Queensland children.

Scouts Queensland is a registered provider for the FairPlay program, which means your voucher can be used towards Scouts membership and activity fees.

**Benefits of Using FairPlay Vouchers for Scouts:**
- Reduces the cost of joining Scouts
- Makes Scouting more accessible to all families
- Can be combined with other financial assistance if eligible
- Supports children's participation in valuable youth development programs
