Play On! Sports Vouchers are a Queensland Government program that helps families with the cost of sport and recreation, including Scouts. Bardon Scouts accepts Play On! vouchers through Scouts Queensland, which is a registered Play On! activity provider.

### What the voucher covers

- **Value:** up to $200 per child
- **Who can get one:** Queensland children aged 5 to 17
- **How many:** one voucher per child each financial year
- **What it pays for:** membership and participation fees. It can't be used for uniforms or equipment.

### Dates for the current round

- Applications are open from 27 July 2026 to 18 December 2026, or until the vouchers run out.
- The government's last day for using a voucher from this round is 18 June 2027.

### How to use it at Bardon Scouts

1. Apply for a voucher on the [Queensland Government Play On! website](https://www.qld.gov.au/recreation/sports/funding/playon/apply).
2. When you have your voucher, let us know through our [contact page](/contact/), and we'll explain how to use it towards your Scouts fees.

Each voucher can only be used once, and any unused amount isn't refunded.
