## Free Trial Visit

Interested in trying Scouting? We welcome families to visit Bardon Scouts for a free trial session to see if Scouting is right for your child.

### Important: Please Contact Us Before Visiting

**Sometimes the regular scout meetings are held away from the Bee St Scout Hall.** For this reason, please make sure you [contact us](/contact/) at least one day before you plan to trial to confirm we will be there and to let us know so we can ensure we are ready to answer any questions you have.

## Required Form

Before your child attends a trial session, please download and complete the Non-Member Activity Indemnity and Release form:

**[Download Form F6 - Non-Member Activity Indemnity and Release (PDF)](/img/F6-Non-Member-Activity-Indemnity-and-Release.pdf)**

Please bring the completed form with you to the trial session.

## Financial Assistance Available

If cost is a concern, Queensland families may be eligible for a $200 [FairPlay voucher](/join/fairplay-voucher/) to help with membership fees. Learn more about this government program on our [FairPlay Voucher page](/join/fairplay-voucher/).
