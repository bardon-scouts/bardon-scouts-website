Your child can come to a Bardon Scouts meeting for free before you decide to join. Fill in the form below at least a day before, and we'll reply to confirm the day and where to meet. Meetings are sometimes held away from the Den.

Before you come, read [Come and Try](/join/come-and-try/) for how a visit works, and fill in [Form F6 (PDF)](/img/F6-Non-Member-Activity-Indemnity-and-Release.pdf) to bring with you.

For anything else, use [Contact Us](/contact/).
