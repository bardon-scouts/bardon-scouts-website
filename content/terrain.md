Scouts Australia uses **Scouts | Terrain** as the online badgework and skills tracking system for all Scouts members. Terrain helps Scouts track their progress, earn badges, and record their achievements throughout their Scouting journey.

**[Access Scouts | Terrain](https://terrain.scouts.com.au/)**

## What is Terrain?

Scouts | Terrain is a dynamic web application that allows Scouts to:

- Track badge progress and achievements
- Submit completed badge work
- Record special interest projects
- View their Scouting journey and milestones
- Access the complete badge and award requirements

## For Parents

Parents play an important role in supporting their child's Scouting journey through Terrain. You can help your Scout by:

- Logging in to view their progress
- Helping them submit completed badge work
- Encouraging them to work toward new badges and awards
- Reviewing their achievements and next goals

### How Parents Access Terrain

Parents login to Terrain using their child's membership credentials:

1. **Website:** Visit [terrain.scouts.com.au](https://terrain.scouts.com.au/)
2. **Login:** Use your child's membership number and password
3. **First-time password:** The initial password is your child's birth date in YYYY-MM-DD format
4. **Multiple children:** If you have more than one child in Scouts, login with the credentials of the child you're currently supporting

Terrain can be accessed through any web browser and can also be saved to your device for easier access.

## Badge Work and Skills

The Terrain system tracks progress across all Scouting badge areas:

- **Outdoor Adventure Skills** - Camping, bushcraft, hiking, navigation
- **STEM & Innovation** - Science, technology, engineering, mathematics
- **Creative Arts** - Arts, crafts, music, drama
- **Growth & Development** - Personal development, leadership, citizenship
- **Creating a Better World** - Community service, environment, global awareness

For more information about specific Scouting skills, visit our **[Skills](/skills/)** section.

## Encouraging Your Scout

Regular engagement with Terrain helps Scouts:

- Set goals and work toward achievements
- See their progress visually
- Stay motivated throughout their Scouting journey
- Develop independence and self-management skills

Parents are encouraged to review Terrain regularly with their Scout, discuss upcoming badges they're interested in, and celebrate completed achievements together.
