## An Overnight Hike for Scouts

Operation NightHawk is an overnight bush hike and one of the largest Scouting youth activities in Queensland, attracting around 1,200 Scouts plus adult leaders and supporters each year. Since its inception in 1982, NightHawk has become one of the largest events in the Queensland Scouting calendar.

Bardon Scouts regularly takes part, giving our Scouts the opportunity to test their skills, challenge themselves, and experience adventure alongside Scouts from across Queensland and Northern NSW.

## The Challenge

Operation NightHawk is more than a hike. It has activity bases throughout the bush where Scouts are tested on their leadership and technical skills.

**What to Expect:**
- **Night-time Navigation** - Hiking through bushland after dark, using navigation skills
- **Skill Bases** - Multiple checkpoints testing mapping, first aid, and Scout skills
- **Leadership Challenges** - Activities that require teamwork and decision-making
- **Technical Skills** - Communication, orienteering, construction, and survival skills
- **General Knowledge** - Scout-specific questions and problem-solving tasks

Each patrol is scored on their performance at the various bases, making it both a learning experience and a friendly competition.

## Event Details

**When:** Traditionally held on the second full weekend in August each year

**Where:** Various locations across the Darling Downs region (rotates annually)
Recent venues have included Crows Nest Showgrounds and Clifton Airfield

**Who:** Scouts only
*Note: This event is specifically for Scouts, not Joeys, Cubs, or Venturers*

**Duration:** Overnight event (Friday evening through Saturday)

**Participants:** Approximately 1,200 Scouts from Queensland and Northern NSW

## A Proud History

Founded in 1982 by Don Masters (Kanga) and Mike Cole (Hawk) of the Toowoomba-based Garraboo District, Operation NightHawk has grown from a local event to Queensland's second-largest Scouting activity.

In 2019, the event was entered in the Outdoors Queensland Awards and won recognition for its outstanding contribution to outdoor youth activities.

The event is organized by Darling Downs Region Scouts with support from:
- Australian Defence Force
- Queensland Ambulance Service
- Queensland Police
- Toowoomba Regional Council
- Local property owners
- Rover Scouts

## Parent Information

### Who can go?
**Scouts only**

This event is specifically designed for the Scout section and is not suitable for Joeys, Cubs, or Venturers.

### What to bring:
- **Torch** (with spare batteries)
- **Hat** for weather protection
- **Backpack** (comfortable for hiking)
- **Snacks** (high-energy food for the hike)
- **2 sets of clothes** (one to hike in, one dry set for after)
- **Sturdy shoes** (broken-in hiking boots or runners)
- **Plate and cup** (for meals)
- Water bottle
- Sleeping bag and mat
- Warm layers (nights can be cold in August)
- Rain jacket
- Personal first aid items and medications
- Scout uniform (as specified by leaders)

### Important Notes:
- Scouts participate in patrols with their unit
- Adult leaders accompany and supervise throughout
- Medical support is available on-site
- Full gear list and details provided closer to the event
- Parent permission and medical forms required
