You can support Bardon Scouts by donating your empty drink containers. Under Queensland's Containers for Change scheme, each eligible container is worth 10c, and the refunds from containers donated to us go to the group.

## How to Donate

Leave your empty containers in the Containers for Change trailer at the front of the Scout Den, 11 Bee Street, Bardon.

Please take the lids off first.

## What Can Be Donated

Most aluminium, glass, plastic, steel and paperboard drink containers from 150ml to 3 litres are eligible. Look for the 10c mark on the label.

If you're not sure about a container, check it on the [Containers for Change website](https://www.containersforchange.com.au/qld/).
