## Making a Difference for Our Environment

Clean Up Australia Day is Australia's largest community-based environmental event, and Bardon Scouts proudly participates each year. This annual event brings together Scouts of all ages—from Joeys to Venturers—to clean up our local parks, waterways, and community spaces while demonstrating our commitment to environmental stewardship and community service.

## Event Details

**When:** First Sunday in March (annually)
**Next Event:** Sunday, 7 March 2027
**Duration:** Typically 2-4 hours
**Where:** Local Bardon area (specific location announced by leaders)
**Who:** Joeys, Cubs, Scouts, Venturers - All sections welcome!

## About Clean Up Australia Day

Founded over 36 years ago by Ian Kiernan AO, Clean Up Australia Day has grown into the nation's largest community environmental event. Each year, over 750,000 volunteers participate in more than 8,000 events across the country, working together to improve and conserve our environment, eliminate litter, and end waste.

Since its inception, more than 23 million wonderful volunteers have contributed to this remarkable movement, making a tangible difference to Australian parks, beaches, waterways, and communities.

**What Makes Clean Up Australia Day Special:**
- **Community Action** - Entire families and communities working together
- **Environmental Impact** - Removing thousands of tonnes of rubbish annually
- **All Ages Welcome** - From young Joeys to adult volunteers
- **National Movement** - Part of Australia's largest environmental event
- **Scouting Values** - Demonstrating care for the environment and community service
- **Local Focus** - Cleaning up our own Bardon neighbourhood
- **Free Participation** - No cost to join, clean-up kits provided

## What We Do on Clean Up Australia Day

Bardon Scouts' Clean Up Australia Day activities typically include:

**Morning Activities:**
- **Meet & Greet** - Gather at designated location in Scout uniform
- **Safety Briefing** - Instructions on safe rubbish collection
- **Equipment Distribution** - Receive gloves, bags, and collection tools
- **Section Allocation** - Groups assigned to specific clean-up areas
- **Clean-Up Time** - Collect rubbish from parks, streets, and public spaces

**Clean-Up Activities:**
- Picking up litter from parks and public areas
- Collecting rubbish from along streets and footpaths
- Clearing waterways and creek banks
- Sorting recyclables from general waste
- Recording types and quantities of rubbish collected
- Properly disposing of collected waste

**Wrap-Up:**
- Return with collected rubbish
- Tally and record waste collected
- Dispose of waste in designated bins
- Return equipment and clean up
- Group reflection on impact made

*Exact activities and location confirmed by Section Leaders before the event*

## Parent Information

### Who can go?
**All sections welcome:**
- **Joeys** - Supervised collection of safe, light rubbish
- **Cubs** - Active participation in clean-up activities
- **Scouts** - Full participation in all clean-up tasks
- **Venturers** - Leadership roles and challenging tasks

**Families encouraged!** Parents, siblings, and families are welcome to participate alongside Scouts.

### What to bring:
- **Scout uniform** (to be worn proudly during the event)
- **Closed-toe shoes** (sturdy shoes or boots—no sandals!)
- **Hat and sunscreen** (outdoor event, sun protection essential)
- **Water bottle** (stay hydrated while working)
- **Hand sanitizer** (for cleaning hands after handling rubbish)
- **Gloves** (if you have your own work gloves, bring them; otherwise provided)
- **Morning tea/snack** (if desired)
- **Positive attitude** ready to make a difference!

**Provided by Clean Up Australia/Leaders:**
- Collection bags for rubbish
- Gloves for handling waste
- Collection tools (litter pickers, grabbers)
- Safety equipment
- Hi-visibility vests (if required)

### Important Information:
- **Registration** - Sign up through your Section Leader
- **Start time** - Typically 9:00am-10:00am start (confirmed before event)
- **Duration** - Usually 2-4 hours
- **Supervision** - Scouts remain with their section and leaders throughout
- **Weather** - Event typically proceeds rain or shine (check with leaders)
- **Safety first** - Don't pick up dangerous items—report them to leaders
- **Current membership** - Financial Scout members for insurance coverage

### Safety Guidelines:
- **Don't touch hazardous items** (glass, syringes, chemicals, sharp objects)
- **Stay with your group** and within designated area
- **Wear gloves** when handling rubbish
- **Wash hands** thoroughly after the event
- **Stay hydrated** and take breaks as needed
- **Report injuries** or concerns to leaders immediately

## The Clean Up Australia Movement

**History:**
Clean Up Australia began in 1989 when Ian Kiernan AO organized a community clean-up of Sydney Harbour. The success led to the first Clean Up Australia Day in 1990, which has grown into the nation's largest environmental event.

**Mission:**
Clean Up Australia inspires and mobilises communities to improve and conserve our environment, eliminate litter, and end waste.

**Year-Round Action:**
While Clean Up Australia Day is held annually on the first Sunday in March, Clean Up Australia encourages people to "Clean Up on any day throughout the year."

**Registration & Support:**
Registered participants receive:
- Free Clean Up kit with materials and resources
- Public liability insurance coverage
- Support and guidance for organizing events
- Access to educational resources
