The 27th Australian Jamboree brings together more than 10,000 Scouts, Venturers, Rovers and Leaders from across Australia for ten days of camping and activities in Elmore, Victoria.

## Event Details

**When:** 29 December 2027 to 7 January 2028, plus travel days
**Duration:** 10 days
**Where:** Elmore Events Centre, Elmore, Victoria
**Who:** Scouts and Venturers (Rovers and Leaders also attend). Who can go is confirmed when applications open.

## Applications Coming Soon

Applications didn't open in September as planned. The Jamboree team and each state's contingent are finishing preparations so the process is ready from the first day.

Scouts Queensland is sending a Queensland contingent. To hear when applications open, register your interest on the [Queensland contingent website](https://aj.scoutsqld.com.au/) and follow [AJ2028 on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/AusJamboree2028).
