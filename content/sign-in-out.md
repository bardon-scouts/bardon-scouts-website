For the safety and security of all our Scouts, Bardon Scouts requires parents or guardians to sign their children in and out at every meeting and event.

### How It Works

**At Every Meeting and Event:**
- A sign-in sheet will be available at the meeting location
- The parent or guardian dropping off must sign their child in upon arrival
- The parent or guardian collecting must sign their child out at the end of the meeting or event

**Why We Require Sign In/Out:**
- Ensures we know exactly which children are present
- Provides accurate attendance records for safety and insurance purposes
- Confirms authorized adults are dropping off and collecting children
- Helps leaders account for all Scouts during activities
- Creates a clear record of arrival and departure times

### Who Must Sign In and Out

**Required for:**
- Joey Scouts
- Cubs
- Scouts
- Venturers

**Venturers - Self Sign-In:**
Venturers who are driving themselves to Scouts may sign themselves in and out if they have prearranged permission from their parents.

### Important Information

**Late Pick-Up**
Please collect your child on time. If you're running late, contact your Section Leader as soon as possible. Leaders cannot leave until all children have been safely collected.

**Safety First**
These procedures are in place to protect your child. We appreciate your cooperation in following the sign-in and sign-out requirements at every meeting and event.

### Questions?

If you have questions about sign-in and sign-out procedures, or need to make special arrangements, please [contact us](/contact/) or speak with your child's Section Leader.
