## Bardon Scouts at Fair on the Green

Bardon Scouts proudly participated in the Milton State School Fair on the Green this year, joining the local community for a fantastic day of fun, fundraising, and friendship.

![Fair on the Green](/img/fair-on-the-green.jpg)

### About Fair on the Green

Fair on the Green is Milton State School's annual community fundraising event, bringing together families from across Brisbane for a day of entertainment and activities. Hosted by the Milton State School P&C Association, the fair has become a beloved fixture on the Brisbane community calendar.

The event typically features:
- Carnival rides and games
- Food trucks and refreshments
- Live performances and entertainment
- Face painting and craft activities
- Emergency service vehicle displays
- Bake sales and second-hand book stalls
- Family-friendly outdoor movie screening

### Our Participation

At this year's Fair on the Green, Bardon Scouts set up a display to showcase what Scouting is all about. We met families from across the local area, answered questions about our programs, and shared stories of adventure and growth.

It was wonderful to see so many children excited about the prospect of joining Scouts, and to connect with families who share our values of community service, outdoor adventure, and youth development.

### Why Community Events Matter

Community events like Fair on the Green are important to Scouts because they help us:
- **Connect with Families:** Meet new families and share what Scouting offers
- **Support Local Schools:** Contribute to fundraising efforts for our local community
- **Demonstrate Our Values:** Show how Scouts are active, engaged community members
- **Build Relationships:** Strengthen ties between Scouts and the broader Milton/Bardon community

### About Milton State School

Milton State School is located in Gregory Park, Milton, and serves the local community with quality education. The school's P&C Association works tirelessly to raise funds for school improvements and resources, with Fair on the Green being their major annual fundraising event.

By participating in community events like this, Bardon Scouts demonstrates our commitment to being active, contributing members of the local Brisbane community.
