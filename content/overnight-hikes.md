Scouts and Venturers hike over two or more days, carrying everything they need in their backpacks and camping overnight between locations. Walks like the [Sunshine Coast Hinterland Great Walk](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/great-walks-sunshine-coast) are typical.

## Event Details

**When:** Usually between April and September (dates announced by Section Leaders)
**Who:** Scouts, Venturers
