Once a year, the whole of Bardon Scouts heads away together for Group Camp. It is the one camp where every section camps side by side, along with the adults who help run the group.

## Event Details

**When:** Once a year in October
**Duration:** Two nights
**Arrive:** Friday at 7pm
**Depart:** Sunday at 2pm
**Who:** Joeys, Cubs, Scouts, Venturers - all sections, plus adult helpers

## Arrival

Arrive at camp at 7pm on Friday. Please make sure your child has had dinner before they arrive. We serve a supper later in the evening.

## Arriving Late or Leaving Early

We use sign in and out sheets at camp. Whenever anyone arrives or leaves, adults and young people alike, record it on the sheet. See [Sign In and Out](/sign-in-out/) for how it works.

## What to Bring

- Scout uniform (worn to and from camp)
- Rucksack
- Sleeping mat (small foam or inflatable)
- Sleeping bag, or blanket and sheets
- Pillow and pillowcase (an inflatable pillow or rolled up clothing is best)
- Trainers or walking shoes
- Hat with a wide brim
- Wet weather jacket
- Warm jacket for night activities
- Socks, underwear, shorts or trousers, and t-shirts or shirts
- Pyjamas
- Toiletries: flannel or sponge, soap, toothbrush and toothpaste, hairbrush or comb
- Towel
- Sunscreen
- Insect repellent
- Personal first aid kit (bandaids and so on)
- Water bottle
- Torch
- Small notepad and pencil
- Mess kit: a bag with a plate, bowl, cup and cutlery

## Sleeping Arrangements

Your child can bring their own tent or use a Bardon Scouts tent. Young people choose their own tent partners, with a maximum of 4 to a tent. Joeys sleep in their parents' tent.

## Departure

We leave camp at 2pm on Sunday. Young people come back to the Den afterwards to help unpack the trailer, which takes about an hour.

If your child used a Bardon Scouts tent and it's wet, please take it home, dry it, check everything is there and in good condition, pack it up and bring it back at the next meeting.

## Activities

- **Pioneering** - building structures with rope and poles
- **Cooking** - preparing meals at camp
- **Swimming**
- **Camping in tents**

## Adult Helpers

Group Camp is open to adult helpers who assist with the running of the group. Adults attending must hold a valid blue card (Working with Children Check).

## More Information

Dates and location are shared by Section Leaders closer to the camp. If you have any questions, speak to your Section Leader or [contact us](/contact/).
