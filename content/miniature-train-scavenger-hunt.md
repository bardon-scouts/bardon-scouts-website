## Event Details

**Status:** Proposed
**When:** To be announced
**Where:** Pine Valley Miniature Railway, Warner
**Who:** Joeys, Cubs, Scouts, Venturers

## What's Planned

A Group event for all sections at the Pine Valley Miniature Railway, run by the model engineering society QSMEE. As well as riding the trains, patrols would compete in a scavenger hunt, capturing photos and completing similar challenges around the site.

## About the Railway

The railway has over 2,000 metres of miniature track on a five acre property. Enclosed shoes are required on site: no thongs or open toed sandals.
