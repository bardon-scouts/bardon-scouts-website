## Five Epic Days of Cub Scout Adventure

Cuboree is Queensland's premier camping experience for Cub Scouts—a massive five-day event held every two to three years that brings together hundreds of Cubs from across the state for non-stop fun, friendship, and outdoor adventure. This unforgettable experience is often a Cub Scout's first extended time away from home and creates memories that last a lifetime.

## Event Details

**When:** Every 2-3 years (Next: Cuboree 2026 - April 9-13, 2026)
**Duration:** 5 days (Thursday to Monday)
**Where:** Baden-Powell Park, Samford, Queensland
**Who:** Cub Scouts from across Queensland

## About Cuboree

Cuboree is a celebration of Cub Scouting on a grand scale. The event brings together over 800 Cub Scouts for five days of adventurous, challenging, and inclusive activities designed to build confidence, independence, and teamwork while ticking off Milestones and developing Outdoor Adventure Skills.

**What Makes Cuboree Special:**
- **State-Wide Event** - Cubs from across Queensland come together
- **Five Full Days** - Extended camping experience with comprehensive programming
- **Subcamp Structure** - Cubs organized into manageable communities
- **Special Interest Areas** - Six themed areas for exploration and achievement
- **Milestone Progress** - Activities designed to support advancement
- **Outdoor Adventure Skills** - Hands-on skill development in nature
- **Lifelong Memories** - Often cited as the highlight of a Cub's Scouting journey

**Event Theme:**
Each Cuboree has a unique theme. Cuboree 2026 is themed "Shake It Up!" - encouraging Cubs to try new things, embrace challenges, and shake up their comfort zones with exciting adventures.

**Environment:**
The park offers a perfect mix of natural bushland and purpose-built Scouting facilities, providing an ideal setting for outdoor adventure, skill development, and the authentic camping experience that defines Cuboree.

## How Cuboree Works

Cuboree operates on a structured system designed to create manageable, supportive communities for Cubs during their five-day adventure.

**Camp Structure:**

**Three Subcamps**
The entire event is divided into three Subcamps, each creating its own community with dedicated facilities:
- Own kitchen and meal preparation areas
- Dedicated recreational zone
- Camping area for Units
- Support facilities and amenities

**Units Within Subcamps**
Each Subcamp contains 6 Units, which are groups of Cubs and leaders working together throughout the event.

**Patrols Within Units**
Each Unit consists of 6 Patrols of 6 Cub Scouts each, creating small teams that work together on activities and challenges.

This tiered structure ensures:
- Manageable group sizes for activities
- Strong patrol bonds and friendships
- Effective supervision and safety
- Community feeling within larger event
- Leadership opportunities at all levels

## Activities & Program

Cuboree delivers a vibrant, action-packed program designed to engage Cubs in outdoor adventure, creative exploration, and skill development.

**Activity Zones:**
Throughout the five days, Cubs rotate through various activity zones including:
- **High adventure activities** - Abseiling, climbing, and challenge courses
- **Aquatic activities** - Swimming, water games, and pool challenges
- **STEM activities** - Science experiments, technology, engineering challenges
- **Creative arts** - Crafts, drama, music, and artistic expression
- **Outdoor skills** - Camping skills, bushcraft, and pioneering
- **Sports and games** - Team sports, wide games, and physical challenges
- **Environmental activities** - Nature exploration and conservation

**Special Interest Areas (SIAs):**
Cubs engage in six Special Interest Areas throughout the event:
1. **Adventure & Sport** - Physical activities and outdoor challenges
2. **Arts & Literature** - Creative expression and storytelling
3. **Creating a Better World** - Community service and global awareness
4. **Environment** - Nature conservation and environmental stewardship
5. **Growth & Development** - Personal development and life skills
6. **STEM & Innovation** - Science, technology, engineering, and mathematics

Cubs complete six SIA projects across at least two different Areas, working toward Milestone achievement and broadening their Scouting experience.

**Off-Site Adventures:**
Cuboree includes exciting excursions beyond Baden-Powell Park:
- **Boondall Wetlands** - Environmental conservation and nature exploration
- **Queensland Air Museum** - Learning Queensland's aviation history
- **Joy flights over Caloundra** - Aerial adventure and unique experiences

**Evening Entertainment:**
Each night features:
- Campfire programs and songs
- Themed entertainment
- Wide games under the stars
- Community gatherings
- Patrol bonding time

## Parent Information

### Who can go?
**Cub Scouts** from across Queensland

Cubs must meet specific age and membership requirements. Eligibility details are provided during registration. This is an event specifically for the Cub Scout section.

### What's included:
Registration includes:
- **5 days of camp** (Thursday to Monday)
- **All meals** provided throughout the event
- **Activities and programs** - Access to all activity zones and excursions
- **Cuboree pack** shipped before the event containing:
  - Long sleeve Cuboree shirt
  - Bucket hat
  - Event scarf (neckerchief)
  - Woggle
  - Event badges
- **Insurance** through Scouts Queensland membership
- **Supervision** by trained leaders throughout the event

### What to bring:
A comprehensive packing list is provided during registration. Typical items include:
- **Sleeping bag** (rated for April temperatures)
- **Sleeping mat** or mattress
- **Backpack or bag** for personal belongings
- **Clothes** for 5 days (weather-appropriate layers)
- **Toiletries** (toothbrush, soap, towel, etc.)
- **Water bottle** (essential for staying hydrated)
- **Sturdy shoes** (closed-toe, comfortable for activities)
- **Hat and sunscreen** (sun protection essential)
- **Rain jacket** (weather can change)
- **Torch/headlamp** with spare batteries
- **Plate, cup, cutlery** (for meals)
- **Personal medications** (clearly labeled)
- **Any required medical equipment**

**What NOT to bring:**
- Electronic devices (phones, tablets, games)
- Valuable items
- Excessive money or jewelry
- Inappropriate items

### Registration & Costs:
- **Registration** opens many months before the event
- **Deposit** required upfront with application
- **Payment plans** available to spread costs
- **Fundraising** - Groups can use fundraising money toward camp fees
- **Withdrawal policies** available on the Cuboree website

**Important:**
- Registration coordinated through your Cub Scout Leader
- Applications close well before the event
- Limited capacity—register early to secure spots

### First Time Away From Home:
For many Cubs, Cuboree is their first extended time away from home. Parents can prepare their child by:
- Talking positively about the adventure
- Practicing independence at home (packing, self-care)
- Attending shorter camps beforehand
- Ensuring they know their leaders and patrol members
- Discussing homesickness coping strategies
- Trusting the experienced leaders and structure

## Safety & Supervision

Cuboree maintains comprehensive safety standards:

✓ **Trained Leaders** - All activities supervised by qualified adult leaders
✓ **High Ratios** - Appropriate adult-to-youth supervision maintained
✓ **Safety Briefings** - Cubs receive safety instructions for all activities
✓ **Medical Support** - First aid trained leaders and medical staff on site
✓ **Emergency Procedures** - Clear protocols for incidents and emergencies
✓ **Activity Risk Assessments** - All activities assessed for hazards
✓ **Secure Site** - Baden-Powell Park provides controlled, safe environment
✓ **Communication** - Leaders have radio/phone communication throughout
✓ **Swimming Safety** - Qualified lifeguards for aquatic activities
✓ **Dietary Management** - Meals planned with dietary requirements considered
✓ **Patrol Accountability** - Regular check-ins and roll calls

**Parent Contact:**
- Emergency contact procedures provided before the event
- Parents can contact event coordinators if needed
- Updates may be provided through social media during the event

## Event History

Cuboree has been a Queensland Cub Scouting tradition for decades, held every two to three years to give as many Cubs as possible the opportunity to attend during their time in the section.

**Recent Events:**
- **Cuboree 2026** - "Shake It Up!" at Baden-Powell Park (April 9-13, 2026)
- **Previous Cuborees** - Held at various locations across Queensland

Each Cuboree builds on the success of previous events while introducing new themes, activities, and opportunities for adventure.

The event has grown significantly over the years, now regularly hosting over 800 participants and offering increasingly diverse programming that reflects modern Scouting values while maintaining outdoor adventure traditions.
