## Challenge Yourself at Rocksports

Rock climbing is an exciting adventure activity that Bardon Scouts offers once or twice a year at Rocksports Indoor Climbing Gym in Fortitude Valley. This indoor climbing experience provides a safe, supervised environment where Scouts can challenge themselves, build confidence, and develop new skills.

## Event Details

**When:** Once or twice per year (dates announced by Section Leaders)
**Time:** Typically 2-3 hour sessions
**Where:** [Rocksports Indoor Climbing](https://www.rocksports.com.au/)
224 Barry Parade, Fortitude Valley QLD 4006

## About Rocksports

Rocksports is Brisbane's first and original indoor rock climbing gym, operating since 1996. Located in a converted brick warehouse in Fortitude Valley, the facility offers a world-class climbing experience just minutes from the Brisbane CBD.

**Facility Features:**
- **30 climbing walls** with over 100 routes
- **8 metre high walls** with colour-coded difficulty levels
- **Auto belay systems** for safe solo climbing
- **Boulder hall** with low-level climbing sections
- **Kid-specific sections** perfect for younger Scouts
- **Professional instruction** and safety briefings
- **All equipment provided** - shoes, harnesses, and chalk included

**Location Benefits:**
- 2 minute walk from Fortitude Valley train station
- 10 minute walk from Brisbane CBD
- Air-conditioned facility with viewing gallery
- Lockers and showers available

[Visit Rocksports Website →](https://www.rocksports.com.au/)

## What to Expect

Rock climbing sessions at Rocksports are designed to be fun, challenging, and safe for all skill levels—from complete beginners to experienced climbers.

**Session Structure:**
1. **Arrival & Registration** - Check in with Section Leaders
2. **Safety Briefing** - Professional staff provide comprehensive safety instruction
3. **Equipment Fitting** - Shoes, harnesses, and chalk provided and fitted
4. **Climbing Time** - Scouts climb with supervision and support
5. **Wrap-up** - Return equipment and debrief

**Climbing Options:**
- **Top-rope climbing** - Climb with a safety rope from above
- **Auto belays** - Solo climbing with automatic safety systems
- **Bouldering** - Low-level climbing without ropes
- **Kid-specific walls** - Age-appropriate routes for younger Scouts

**Skill Levels:**
Routes are colour-coded from beginner to advanced, ensuring everyone can find challenges at their own level. Staff are always on hand to offer advice, encouragement, and assistance.

## Why Scouts Love Rock Climbing

**Physical Challenge**
Rock climbing develops strength, coordination, flexibility, and endurance in a fun, engaging way.

**Mental Development**
Climbing builds problem-solving skills, focus, and mental resilience as Scouts plan their route and overcome obstacles.

**Confidence Building**
Conquering a challenging wall or reaching new heights creates a sense of achievement that builds self-confidence.

**Teamwork & Encouragement**
Scouts cheer each other on, share techniques, and celebrate each other's successes—building camaraderie and teamwork.

**Safe Adventure**
Professional instruction, quality equipment, and controlled environment make indoor climbing a safe introduction to the sport.

**Skill Development**
Learn proper climbing techniques, knot tying, safety procedures, and risk assessment—skills applicable beyond the climbing wall.

## Parent Information

### Who can go?
Rock climbing events are typically open to all sections, though this may vary by event:
- Joeys - Kid-specific walls and bouldering
- Cubs - All climbing options with supervision
- Scouts - All climbing options
- Venturers - All climbing options including advanced routes

*Specific section participation will be confirmed by your Section Leader for each event*

### What to bring:
- **Comfortable athletic clothing** (pants or leggings recommended, avoid loose clothing)
- **Socks** (required for climbing shoes)
- **Water bottle** (climbing is thirsty work!)
- **Hair tie** (if you have long hair)
- **Light snack** for before or after climbing

**Equipment Provided:**
- Climbing shoes
- Harnesses
- Chalk
- All safety equipment

### Important Requirements:
- **Supervision** - Scouts must stay with the group and follow leader instructions
- **Safety first** - All Scouts must complete the safety briefing
- **Physical activity** - Climbing is physically demanding; Scouts should be in good health
- **Follow instructions** - Staff and leader directions must be followed at all times
- **Current Scout membership** - Must be a financial member for insurance coverage

## Safety & Supervision

Rocksports maintains the highest safety standards, and Bardon Scouts adds additional supervision:

✓ **Professional Instruction** - Rocksports staff provide comprehensive safety briefings
✓ **Quality Equipment** - All climbing gear meets safety standards and is regularly inspected
✓ **Scout Leader Supervision** - Bardon Scout Leaders supervise throughout the session
✓ **Safety Systems** - Auto belays and top-rope systems provide redundant safety
✓ **Trained Staff** - Rocksports staff are experienced and trained in climbing safety
✓ **Beginner Friendly** - Staff monitor the gym and offer assistance to beginners

**No Experience Required:**
All beginners receive full instruction before climbing. Staff wander the gym offering advice and assistance throughout the session.
