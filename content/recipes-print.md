To save your cookbook as a PDF, press **Print / Save as PDF**, then choose **Save as PDF** (or **Microsoft Print to PDF**) as the printer. On a phone, use the share or print option and choose to save the file.

Each recipe starts on a new page only when it won't fit on the rest of the page, so short recipes can share a page.
