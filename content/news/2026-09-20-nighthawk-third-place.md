Congratulations to the Scout section for placing third at Operation NightHawk! With more than 1,000 Scouts competing from around Queensland and NSW, it is an amazing achievement to place third.

Operation NightHawk is Queensland's premier overnight Scout hike, held annually on the Darling Downs. This legendary event challenges Scouts with night-time navigation, skill bases, and leadership activities. Our Scouts demonstrated exceptional teamwork, navigation skills, and determination throughout the challenging course.

This outstanding result reflects the hard work and dedication of our Scouts, as well as the support and training provided by our Scout Leaders. We're incredibly proud of this achievement and look forward to seeing our Scouts continue to excel at future events!
