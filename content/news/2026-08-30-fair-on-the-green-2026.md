Bardon Scouts erected one of our classic Queenslander tents at the Milton State School Fete and it was great to meet all the parents!

Our Queenslander tent drew plenty of visitors. We talked with families from the local area, shared information about our Scouting programs, and connected with both current Scout families and those interested in joining.

Thank you to everyone who stopped by to say hello, and to our volunteers who helped set up and staff the tent throughout the day. Events like this are a great reminder of the strong connection between Bardon Scouts and our local community!
