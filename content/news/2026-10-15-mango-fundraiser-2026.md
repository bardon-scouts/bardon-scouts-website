Orders are now open for the 2026 Mango Fundraiser! Get your space ready for your tray (or five) of mangoes.

Our annual mango fundraiser sells trays of Special Bowen mangoes from North Queensland, ready to collect from the Scout Den in late November or early December, depending on the harvest.

Order by 30 October 2026. The money raised supports Bardon Scouts programs, equipment, and activities throughout the year.
