The 2026 Mango Fundraiser is coming soon in October! Get your space ready for your tray (or five) of mangoes.

Our annual mango fundraiser sells trays of Kensington Pride mangoes from North Queensland farms, ready to collect from the Scout Den in early December.

Orders will open soon. All proceeds support Bardon Scouts programs, equipment, and activities throughout the year.
