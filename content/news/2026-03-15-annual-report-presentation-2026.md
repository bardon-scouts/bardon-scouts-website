Thank you to those that attended the Annual Report Presentation (ARP) night!

It was wonderful to see so many parents, leaders, and community members come together to review our achievements from the past year. We presented reports on our finances, equipment, facilities, and committee activities, and heard fantastic updates from each of our sections - Joeys, Cubs, Scouts, and Venturers.

The ARP is an important part of our group's governance and transparency, and your attendance and participation help make Bardon Scouts stronger. Thank you to everyone who contributed to the discussions, asked questions, and supported our volunteers and committee members. We look forward to another great year ahead!
