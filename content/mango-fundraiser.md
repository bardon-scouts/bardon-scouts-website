## Mangoes are back!

**Fresh Special Bowen mangoes from North Queensland are a delicious way to support Bardon Scouts!**

Summer just wouldn't be summer without mangoes!

Each year, Bardon Scouts sells trays of Special Bowen mangoes, grown in North Queensland and supplied fresh for our annual mango fundraiser.

They're perfect for sharing with family and friends, taking along to a summer BBQ, or simply keeping a few aside for yourself. And by ordering through Bardon Scouts, you're helping support our local Scout group. Best of all, when you buy through our Mango Fundraiser, you're getting fruit that comes directly from the farm to you. That means no long storage times or artificial ripening rooms, just fresh, delicious mangoes at their best.

![A 7kg tray of mangoes in a Mango Fundraiser box](/img/mango-tray.jpg)

## 🥭 Get your mangoes!

**$35 per 7kg tray**

Each tray contains approximately 12 to 23 mangoes, depending on size.

Order one tray or stock up for summer; there's no limit to how many trays you can order!
