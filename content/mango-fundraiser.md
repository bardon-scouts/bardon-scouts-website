## About Kensington Pride Mangoes

Kensington Pride is Australia's most beloved mango variety, accounting for over 80% of the country's commercial mango market. Known for their exceptional flavour, these mangoes are picked during the premium North Queensland harvest season in December when they're at their peak quality.

## Storage & Ripening Tips

**At Room Temperature:**
Allow mangoes to ripen at room temperature for 2-5 days until they give slightly when gently squeezed and have a sweet aroma.

**Refrigeration:**
Once ripe, store in the refrigerator for up to 5 days to maintain freshness.

**Freezing:**
Peel, dice, and freeze for smoothies and cooking—frozen mango can last several months!
