## Overnight Navigation Adventure

KnightMoves is an exciting overnight navigation and camping activity organized annually by Brisbane North Region for Scout and Venturer sections. This challenging event combines night-time navigation, bushwalking, activity bases, and camping in a fun, adventure-packed format that tests skills, builds confidence, and creates unforgettable memories.

## Event Details

**When:** Late July or early August (annually)
**Time:** Saturday afternoon through Sunday morning
**Where:** Various Scouts Queensland campsites (location changes yearly)
**Who:** Scouts and Venturers

## About KnightMoves

Now in its 14th year, KnightMoves is Brisbane North Region's premier overnight navigation event, regularly attracting over 250 Scouts and Venturers from across the region. The event provides youth members with the opportunity to develop navigation and bushwalking skills in a safe, controlled environment during a challenging overnight hike.

**What Makes KnightMoves Special:**
- **Night Navigation** - Navigate through the bush using maps, compasses, and teamwork
- **Activity Bases** - Complete challenges at themed activity stations along the route
- **Patrol Competition** - Work as a patrol to earn points and compete for awards
- **Leadership Development** - Scouts and Venturers lead their patrols independently
- **Overnight Adventure** - Camp overnight and experience the bush at night
- **Regional Event** - Meet Scouts and Venturers from across Brisbane North

**Event Scale:**
- 200-270 Scouts participating annually
- 30-50 Venturers joining the challenge
- Multiple themed activity bases throughout the route
- 25 navigation route markers to find and identify
- Adult leaders and Rovers providing safety support

## How KnightMoves Works

KnightMoves combines navigation challenges with activity bases in an overnight adventure format.

**Event Structure:**

**Saturday Afternoon/Evening**
- **Arrival & Setup** - Patrols arrive at the campsite and set up base camp
- **Opening Ceremony** - Welcome, safety briefing, and event introduction
- **Navigation Briefing** - Receive instruction booklet with navigation details
- **Start** - Patrols depart on their overnight navigation route

**Saturday Night**
- **Navigation Challenge** - Find and identify 25 route markers using maps and compasses
- **Activity Bases** - Complete challenges at themed "islands" or stations
- **Patrol Independence** - Navigate and make decisions as a team
- **Leader Support** - Adult leaders monitor safety throughout the night

**Sunday Morning**
- **Return to Base Camp** - Patrols complete their route and return
- **Breakfast & Rest** - Meals and recovery time
- **Closing Ceremony** - Awards for best navigation, best base performance, and participation
- **Pack Up & Departure** - Break camp and head home

**Navigation Challenge:**
Each patrol receives a detailed instruction booklet (themed creatively each year—past themes include "Pirates Guide to the Caribbean Sea" and space exploration). Patrols must:
- Locate 25 route markers along their designated route
- Decode unique codes at each marker
- Navigate to themed activity bases ("Islands" or "Planets")
- Earn points for successful navigation and base completion

**Activity Bases:**
Scattered throughout the navigation route, activity bases provide:
- Skill challenges (first aid, knots, pioneering)
- Team challenges and problem-solving
- Physical activities
- Leadership scoring
- Technical skills assessment
- Fun and creative themed activities

## Parent Information

### Who can go?
**Scouts** and **Venturers**

KnightMoves is designed for Brisbane North Region's Scout and Venturer sections. This overnight navigation event requires participants to have basic map and compass skills and physical fitness for night hiking.

### What to bring:
- **Backpack** (comfortable for hiking with gear)
- **Sleeping bag** (rated for overnight temperatures)
- **Sleeping mat** or mattress
- **Tent** (patrols typically share tents)
- **Torch/headlamp** (with spare batteries—essential for night navigation!)
- **Compass** (for navigation)
- **Water bottles** (hydration essential—2-3 litres capacity)
- **Food** (Saturday dinner, snacks for overnight, Sunday breakfast)
- **Plate, cup, cutlery** (for meals)
- **Warm clothing** (temperatures drop at night)
- **Rain jacket** (weather can change)
- **Sturdy hiking boots** (broken in, comfortable for night hiking)
- **Hat and sunscreen** (for daytime activities)
- **Personal first aid kit** (bandaids, personal medications)
- **Whistle** (emergency signaling)
- **Personal hygiene items** (toothbrush, towel, etc.)
- **Change of clothes** (for Sunday)
- **Scout uniform** (for opening/closing ceremonies)

### Important Requirements:
- **Navigation Skills** - Scouts and Venturers should have basic map and compass knowledge
- **Physical Fitness** - Ability to hike several kilometers at night with a backpack
- **Patrol Participation** - Work as part of a patrol throughout the event
- **Current Membership** - Financial Scout or Venturer member for insurance coverage
- **Safety Compliance** - Follow all leader instructions and safety procedures
- **Communication** - Carry whistle for emergency signaling

### Travel & Logistics:
- **Group Travel** - Bardon Scouts travels together to the campsite
- **Transport** - Details provided by Section Leaders (typically bus or carpool)
- **Drop-off** - Saturday afternoon at designated meeting point
- **Pick-up** - Sunday late morning/early afternoon at campsite or meeting point
- **Duration** - Overnight event (approximately 24 hours)

## Event Safety & Supervision

KnightMoves maintains comprehensive safety standards for overnight navigation:

✓ **Risk Assessment** - Thorough route planning and hazard identification before event
✓ **Safety Briefings** - All participants receive detailed safety instructions
✓ **Adult Supervision** - Trained adult leaders monitor checkpoints and activity bases
✓ **Rover Support** - Rovers assist with route monitoring and participant safety
✓ **Emergency Procedures** - Clear protocols for emergencies and incidents
✓ **First Aid Coverage** - First aid trained leaders and medical supplies available
✓ **Communication Systems** - Leaders equipped with radios and emergency contacts
✓ **Checkpoint Monitoring** - Regular patrol check-ins at route markers and bases
✓ **Weather Monitoring** - Event can be modified or postponed for severe weather
✓ **Controlled Environment** - Navigation routes planned within defined, safe areas

**Night Navigation Safety:**
- Routes pre-checked for hazards
- Route markers clearly marked and monitored
- Leaders positioned at key checkpoints
- Emergency extraction procedures in place
- Patrols equipped with whistles for signaling
- Minimum patrol size requirements maintained

## Awards & Recognition

KnightMoves recognizes patrol achievement through various awards:

**Best Navigation**
Awarded to the patrol with the highest navigation score (finding route markers, correct codes, timing)

**Best Activity Base Performance**
Awarded to patrols excelling at activity challenges throughout the route

**Patrol Spirit**
Recognition for teamwork, encouragement, and positive attitude

**Participation Awards**
All participants receive recognition for completing the challenge

**Points System:**
- Navigation earns the most points for patrols
- Activity bases contribute to overall scoring
- Leadership and teamwork assessed at activity stations
- Technical skills evaluated throughout the event
