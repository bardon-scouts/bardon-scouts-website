## About Scouts Queensland Air Activities

The event would use the [Scouts Queensland Air Activities](https://member.scoutsqld.com.au/air-activities/) program. Its activity days include a flight in a Cessna 172, a look over an aircraft, and flight simulators. The program also runs a half-day Learn to Fly session, model gliders and drone flying. It operates from airfields at Archerfield, Redcliffe, Toowoomba and Maryborough.

Which of these Bardon Scouts would take part in, and where, hasn't been decided.
