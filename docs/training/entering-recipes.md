# Entering a recipe in the CMS

For leaders adding or editing a recipe on the Recipes page (`/recipes/`). The short version is at the top of the Recipes collection in the CMS.

1. Log in at https://bardonscouts.org.au/admin/ and open **Recipes**. Choose **New Recipe**, or open one to edit it.
2. Fill in the **Title** and a one-line **Description** (what it is and how it's cooked).
3. **Tags:** words people can filter by, such as camp, dinner, breakfast, hike or fundraiser. Use lower case and reuse tags you can see on the Recipes page, so the same tag isn't listed twice.
4. **Serves:** how many people the amounts feed. Scaling (planned) will start from this number.
5. **Prep time** and **Cook time:** as you'd say them, such as "15 minutes".
6. **Cooked on:** tick each way it can be cooked. If you need one that isn't on the list, ask for it to be added.
7. **Dietary notes:** general notes such as "Vegetarian" or "Contains nuts". People still check allergies for their own group, and the Recipes page says so.
8. **Image** (optional): a photo of the food. No photos where children can be identified.

## Ingredients

Add one row per ingredient, in the order you use them.

| Field | What to put | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Amount | A number. Use decimals for parts: 0.5 shows as ½, 0.25 as ¼ | 2 |
| Unit | Pick from the list, or leave empty for whole items | cup |
| Name | Singular | self-raising flour |
| Plural name | Only if it isn't the name plus "s" | potatoes |
| Note | Anything else | sifted |
| Group | Optional heading for part of the recipe. Keep rows with the same group together | Sauce |

- Things with no amount, such as "salt and pepper, to taste": leave Amount and Unit empty, put "salt and pepper" in Name and "to taste" in Note.
- A pack size goes in the note: Amount 1, Unit can, Name diced tomatoes, Note 400 g.
- Whole items: Amount 2, no Unit, Name onion. The site shows "2 onions".

Keeping amounts and units in their own fields is what will let the site scale a recipe to a different number of people.

## Method

Write numbered steps in the Method box. Leave amounts out of the steps ("add the stock", not "add 500 ml of stock"), because amounts in the text can't be scaled. Times and temperatures are fine. You can use headings (such as "Rice") to split a long method into parts.

## Saving

Save, then set the entry's status to **Ready** in the CMS's workflow so it's published. It appears on the Recipes page once the site has rebuilt, after a minute or two.
